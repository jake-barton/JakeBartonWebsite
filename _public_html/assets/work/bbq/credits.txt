## Film and design references

- *Fried Green Tomatoes* (1991), directed by Jon Avnet; screenplay by Fannie Flagg and Carol Sobieski. [AFI synopsis and credits](https://catalog.afi.com/Film/58891-FRIED-GREEN-TOMATOES). The game turns the barbecue revelation into an imagined nightmare.
- Creative Assembly, [Building Fear in Alien: Isolation](https://gdcvault.com/play/1021852/Building-Fear-in-Alien) and [bringing the creature to life](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2014/10/07/creative-assembly-brought-iconic-monster-life-alien-isolation): references for one persistent threat, sensory cues and hiding/searching. No assets or code copied.
- Puppet Combo, [Murder House](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1064460/Murder_House/): reference for low-resolution slasher presentation and stalking. No assets copied.

- Wright Bagwell, [Dead Space 2 creative-director interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/audio/the-new-theory-of-horror-i-dead-space-2-i-s-creative-director-speaks): timing, removing distractions around scare moments, and silence/sound contrast. Applied as ambient ducking, a delayed movement cue, and a brief capture without explanatory text.
- Thomas Grip, [Top Three Questions about SOMA](https://frictionalgames.com/2015-09-top-three-questions-about-soma/): varied tension and horror tied to the subject. Applied by separating discovery, hunting, search and withdrawal; Frank is the story's returning violence.

Title composition references: [Silent Hill 2 menu](https://www.silenthillmemories.net/sh2/playing_the_game_en.htm), [Resident Evil 7 title screen](https://wall.alphacoders.com/big.php?i=797011), and [Murder House official Nintendo screenshots](https://ec.nintendo.com/AU/en/titles/70010000045498). Used to study visual hierarchy and sparse menu copy; no reference imagery is included in the game.
