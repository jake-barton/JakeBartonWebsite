# Portfolio work

Read and follow [PORTFOLIO_STYLE_GUIDE.md](PORTFOLIO_STYLE_GUIDE.md) when changing portfolio pages, media, shared components, or Studio templates. Barton Design Solutions and Everybody Golf are the layout references.

Keep project titles and descriptions plain and factual. Use authentic project assets, retain accurate role and team credits, and keep unapproved prototypes unpublished.

After site changes, run `./sync_to_hostinger.sh` to update the local deployment folders. Do not treat that local sync as a live deployment.
